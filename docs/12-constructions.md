## Construction rules (Phase-1)

- Exactly **one** construction per craft.
- Slot conflicts (two body pieces) are **out of scope** until multi-slot loadouts exist — Phase-1 = one garment per adventure.
- `con_armor` + high MOB materials is a valid “wrong but interesting” clumsy knight outcome.
