# Vision

## Pitch
Roguelike deckbuilder where you are a **magical clothier**. You do not fight — **your clothing does**.

You craft magical garments for adventurers who face dangerous monsters and environments. Success or failure depends on the **stats + tag synergies** of the equipment you create.

## Design priorities (stamped)
- Keep systems **accountable and linear** for easier balancing and debugging
- Favor **data-driven tag logic** over narrative branching
- Support **"wrong but interesting"** outcomes without making them the everyday expectation
- Art strategy optimized for AI generation: modular icons + portraits + selective full illustrations for discoveries only
