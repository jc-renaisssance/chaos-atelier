# Art strategy

## AI-friendly pipeline
- **Modular** material / construction / enchantment **icons**
- **Outlook outcomes**: construction × synergy (pos **and** neg; neg orders higher) — [15-outlook-gen-list](15-outlook-gen-list.md)
- **Player-owner atelier** chrome — [16-shop-owners](16-shop-owners.md); Phase-1 = basic clothier only
- **Customer portraits**
- **Selective full illustrations** for Encyclopedia discoveries only

## Outlook contract (Jonathan lock)
- Combination art per construction × outlook synergy
- Plain/default when nothing fires
- Negatives included on the ladder **above** related positives
- Rare/legendary crafts never show neg outlook (negs blocked in resolver)
- Phase-1 gen ask: **50** garment + optional `atelier_own_basic`

## Locks
- Stamp icon grain + tag color grammar before mass content
- No gens on locked icons
- Hard gen budget before first batch
- Placeholders OK in Client until sockets stamp
- **No multi-owner atelier art** until stamped Later

## Out of scope for Phase-1 art
Boss full sets, multi-owner atelier sets, full 408-cell matrix, everyday Encyclopedia illos.

**M2 market shelf icons:** `staff_*` / `artifact_*` faces landed at `assets/m2-market/` (#48 / #49). **Next (Design-ready 2026-10-20, Jonathan stamp):** six existing `sk_*` shelf faces and the tab-chrome A/B in [32](32-shop-and-events.md); portrait sockets for the 12 M1 adventurers in [29](29-adventurer-catalog.md). Default chrome stamp is **A** (no chrome gens). Ping **Harribel before gens**. No shiny PixelLab overlay gens (code-only tint on enchanted materials only). Skills, staffs, and gear never get shiny gens. Owner chrome / shopkeeper portraits stay out. No posture sheets, no M2-job people, no Scrap Duelist portraits.
